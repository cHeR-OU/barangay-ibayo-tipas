Barangay Services

Mag-apply ng mga dokumento online upang mabilis na maihanda ang inyong mga papeles bago kunin sa Barangay Hall.

Barangay Clearance

Request clearance for employment, business, postal ID, police clearance, or legal purposes.

• Valid ID with Ibayo-Tipas address
• Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
• Bayad: ₱50.00 (O Libre para sa First-Time Jobseeker)

Certificate of Residency

Proof of residency for school admission, banking, utility applications, and government transactions.

• Valid ID o Voter's Certification
• Proof of billing o lease contract
• Bayad: ₱30.00

Certificate of Indigency

Para sa tulong-medikal sa Taguig TLC, hospital assistance, DSWD, scholarship, at Public Attorney's Office (PAO).

• 1 Valid ID / Patunay ng Paninirahan
• Hospital bill o school requirement
• 100% LIBRE (Walang Bayad)

Health Services

Free consultations, child immunization (EPI), dental services, prenatal care, at maintenance medicine sa health center.

• Lunes – Biyernes, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
• Hotline: 0961 734 0886
• Katabi ng Barangay Hall

First-Time Jobseeker (RA 11261)

Libreng sertipikasyon para sa mga fresh graduate o unang beses maghahanap ng trabaho upang makakuha ng libreng government clearances.

• School ID / Diploma / TOR
• Signed Oath of Undertaking
• 100% LIBRENG SERTIPIKASYON

Waste Collection & Sanitation

Regular garbage collection, anti-dengue misting, at Oplan Linis Estero across all purok areas in Ibayo-Tipas.

• Regular schedule bawat purok
• Huwag magtapon ng basura sa ilog
• I-report ang baradong kanal sa e-Reklamo
